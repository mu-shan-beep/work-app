# 整体 UI 装饰图

使用内置 imagegen 新生成两张透明装饰图，作用于界面背景，不作为任务图片或任务缩略图。

## ui-header-decor

素材：[ui-header-decor.png](ui-header-decor.png)

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: actual decorative artwork for the overall background of a refined productivity app, NOT task thumbnails, NOT an icon, NOT a UI screenshot. Transparent background is essential. Cohesive premium paper-cut editorial illustration, softly layered translucent paper and fine organic contour lines, elegant and restrained. Palette: dusty cool blue, grey lavender, sage grey, porcelain white, tiny muted silver-blue highlights. No yellow, no orange, no text, no logo, no book, no stationery, no desk, no photo, no device, no repeated icons, no rectangular background, no frame, no heavy shadows. Intended as peripheral UI decoration behind the interface, so airy flowing shapes and graceful negative space, no dense central subject. Create one wide landscape decorative composition, aspect ratio approximately 3:1. A graceful small cluster of flowing blue and lilac paper ribbons intertwined with stylized translucent cloud contours and a few slender sage leaves, concentrated on the RIGHT HALF, tapering left into a few very fine curved contour lines. 2 or 3 tiny four-point star accents only. Shape should feel like a light breeze, calm imagination and creative flow, not a collection of recognizable app symbols. Leave the left third mostly transparent. Soft sculptural layering, beautiful sophisticated silhouette, readable at 350px wide.
